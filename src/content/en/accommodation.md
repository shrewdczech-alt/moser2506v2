Experience Karlovy Vary from the comfort of our modern, fully equipped
one-bedroom apartment (48 m²). Set within the luxurious, gated Moser
Residence, our apartment offers the ultimate blend of tranquility, privacy
and spa-style relaxation — all without leaving the building.

### Your comfort in the apartment

- **Sleep and relaxation:** A separate bedroom with a comfortable double bed
  and a living room with a spacious sofa bed. Naturally, fragrant bed linen
  and clean towels are fully prepared for you and included in the price.
- **Morning coffee on the balcony:** The apartment also features its own
  private balcony. A coffee machine will be waiting for you right in the
  kitchen, so you can enjoy your favourite first cup of the day in the
  fresh air.
- **Fully equipped kitchen:** Everything you need to prepare your own meals
  is right here. There are all necessary dishes, a microwave and a dishwasher
  that will take care of the cleanup for you.
- **Entertainment and work:** Fast free Wi-Fi connection and a smart TV are
  a matter of course.

### Wellness and services directly on site

- **Spa just a few floors down:** Directly within the complex you can use the
  indoor swimming pool and warm up in the sauna in the evening hours. For a
  perfectly romantic experience, a private whirlpool can be booked for an
  extra fee.
- **For active guests:** The facilities also include a fully equipped gym.
- **Safety and worry-free parking:** The entire complex is fenced and guarded
  by a 24-hour reception. The apartment comes with your own reserved parking
  space, which is a huge advantage in Karlovy Vary.

### Great location

Perfectly located, the residence puts the best of Karlovy Vary within easy
reach:

- **The Colonnade:** The famous spa zone is just 2 km away — a perfect
  destination for a pleasant walk, or you can easily get there by a quick
  public transport connection.
- **Moser Glassworks and Varyáda:** Right in the neighbourhood you can visit
  the world-famous glassworks. The largest shopping centre, Varyáda, is also
  just a few minutes' walk away.
- **Bike path along the Ohře River:** A beautiful and flat bike path (also
  great for inline skating) starts just a short distance from the apartment.
  Following the river, it will take you all the way to the historic Loket
  Castle.
