heading: Frequently asked questions
subheading: Important information for guests
items:
  - question: When can I check in?
    answer: >-
      You can check in from 16:00 on the day of arrival. The online check-in
      must be completed before arrival. For legal and organisational
      reasons, the online check-in must be completed and the accommodation
      payment fully settled before the keys can be handed over.
  - question: When do I have to check out?
    answer: >-
      On the final day of your stay, please vacate the apartment by 10:00 so
      we have enough time to perfectly prepare everything for the next guests.
  - question: Is parking included?
    answer: >-
      Yes. The apartment comes with its own reserved parking space inside the
      gated residence, so you do not have to look for street parking in
      Karlovy Vary.
  - question: Can I use the pool, sauna and gym?
    answer: >-
      Yes. They are located directly within the Moser Residence complex and
      are available to you as a guest of the apartment. On weekdays the pool
      is only accessible from 12:00, and unfortunately children under 15 may
      only use the pool until 17:30. A private whirlpool can be booked for an
      extra fee.
  - question: Are bed linen and towels included?
    answer: >-
      Yes. Clean bed linen and towels are prepared for you and included in the
      price, as is the final cleaning. However, we do ask you to leave the
      apartment as you found it: please empty the fridge, put clean dishes
      back in the cupboards, strip the bed linen, and leave the used towels
      in one place.
