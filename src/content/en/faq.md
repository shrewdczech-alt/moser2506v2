heading: Frequently asked questions
subheading: Important information for guests
items:
  - question: When can I check in?
    answer: >-
      You can check in from 16:00 on the day of arrival. For safety and
      organisational reasons, the online check-in must be completed and the
      accommodation payment fully settled before the keys (lock codes) can be
      handed over.
  - question: When do I have to check out?
    answer: >-
      On the final day of your stay, please vacate the apartment by 10:00 so
      we have enough time to perfectly prepare everything for the next guests.
  - question: Is parking included?
    answer: >-
      Yes. The apartment comes with its own reserved parking space inside the
      gated residence, so you do not have to look for street parking in
      Karlovy Vary.
  - question: Can I use the pool, sauna and gym?
    answer: >-
      Yes. They are located directly within the Moser Residence complex and
      are available to you as a guest of the apartment. A private whirlpool
      can be booked for an extra fee.
  - question: Are bed linen and towels included?
    answer: >-
      Yes. Clean bed linen and towels are prepared for you and included in the
      price, as is the cleaning.
